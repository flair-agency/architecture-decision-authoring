# Contributing

Thanks for helping shape this design project. It is currently in the design and pre-pilot phase; contributions are proposals and do not by themselves establish an adopted product or architecture decision.

## Before contributing

- Check the [open issues](https://github.com/flair-agency/architecture-decision-authoring/issues) and [project milestones](https://github.com/flair-agency/architecture-decision-authoring/milestones) for context.
- For a substantial change, open or join an issue first so the question and scope can be discussed.
- Keep proposals small and explain the problem, relevant evidence, alternatives considered, and unresolved choices.
- Preserve attribution and license information for any third-party material. Its original license continues to apply.

## Proposing changes

Use a pull request to present the proposed change and its rationale. Identify assumptions and owner decisions that remain unresolved. Review and merge do not imply that an architecture decision has been adopted; adoption belongs to the authorized owner and follows the project's decision process.

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). There is no service-level agreement or guaranteed response time for issues or pull requests.
