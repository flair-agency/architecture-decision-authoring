# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, insults, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact route listed on the repository's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
