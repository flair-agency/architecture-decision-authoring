# Winter production brief
Author: Mira, production coordinator. Revision 2, 2026-09-20.
P1. The show uses two rehearsal rooms and one projection workstation in each. A cue list is text plus image filenames; image distribution is out of scope for this proposal.
P2. The director currently emails revised cue lists. During four observed rehearsals, one desk used yesterday's list on two occasions. We have no estimate of the resulting delay.
P3. The production runs from 2026-11-02 through 2026-12-12. Only the director releases a cue list for a rehearsal. Suggestions by performers are not releases.
P4. Lighting, fire alarms, emergency announcements, and the safety interlock are controlled separately and must not be connected to this prototype.
