# Remote trial authorization
Author: Nessa, board chair. Dated 2026-09-10, resolution BR-62.
R1. The board authorizes a remote-contributor trial for interviews recorded from 2027-03-01 through 2027-04-30, involving adult contributors only. Board minutes BR-62 record adoption of this rule. That evidence is supplied as text here; no independent authentication is provided.
R2. For the trial, delete raw interview uploads 72 hours after first broadcast. Unbroadcast uploads are deleted seven days after upload. Published edited programs follow the separate existing archive schedule, which is out of scope.
R3. Exception: retain an upload subject to a documented rights dispute until the dispute is resolved; after resolution the board must decide a deletion deadline. Do not invent that deadline.
R4. Only a board vote may amend R1-R3. The trial coordinator can schedule interviews but cannot amend retention rules. The board will review the trial on 2027-04-15; this review does not end the trial early.
