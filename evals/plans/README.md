# Evaluation plans

This directory holds reviewable run-plan proposals and their later owner-authorized revisions. A plan's status, a completed field, a commit, or a merge is not authorization. Only an explicit owner outcome recorded with evidence for a specifically identified plan revision can adopt it or freeze Phase A. Phase B is a separate owner decision and remains unavailable until fresh held-out inputs and adjudicated expectations exist.

- [Pilot 001 Phase A proposal](pilot-001.md) — proposed conditions for owner review; not adopted or frozen. Phase B has not started.
- [Pilot 002 current-Skill preparation](pilot-002-preparation.md) — proposed public rehearsal and actual-owner observation before a current-candidate Phase A; no held-out generation or condition waiver. The [observation procedure](pilot-002-owner-observation.md) is prepared, not conducted. The [CLI preflight record](pilot-002-cli-preflight.md) separates performed non-generating checks from the proposed minimum rehearsal. Original Pilot 001/protocol/rubric remain unchanged.

Keep superseded plan revisions, owner evidence, execution records, and original results; do not overwrite them. Follow the [comparative pilot protocol](../protocol.md) and [results guidance](../results/README.md).
