# Pilot 002 — actual-owner conversation observation

**Status: Prepared procedure; not conducted.** This uses an exposed fictional case under [Pilot 002 preparation](pilot-002-preparation.md). It is exploratory evidence about the current Skill's interaction with a human, not held-out comparison, fictional-policy adoption or proof of general effectiveness. No observations or responses are prefilled.

## Session boundary

Propose one 20–30-minute owner session after the public paired rehearsal's raw outputs are available. Availability and actual duration are unknown. Use the same public case, sources and exact Proposal identities. The owner is the actual human using ADA; fictional decision-owner evidence stays labelled fictional. No Gatekeeper, CI, required check, package export, canonical edit or PR action is needed.

The coordinator keeps the arm mapping private until the initial neutral-output assessments are recorded. Explain that output wording may disclose the arm and that familiarity/order effects cannot be eliminated in this small session. Keep the initial paired-output scoring separate from subsequent guided interaction. Do not repair outputs before initial scoring or retroactively improve their scores using later conversation.

Use plain task notes in the existing evaluation convention. No new application, wizard, outcome schema or persistent runtime is required. Keep source packets, raw outputs and negative results. Retain observations privately by default; do not publish owner quotes or transcripts without separate authorization.

## Observe, without scripting the owner's conclusions

| Task | Ask the human to do | Record only what actually occurs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial assessment | Read each neutral output and its sources; identify the active decision, relevant existing decision and remaining owner choice in their own words. | Active intervals, material corrections with output/source locations, missed choices, unsupported claims, unnecessary questions, source fidelity and suspected arm assignment. |
| Brief and full Proposal | Use the current Skill's brief and available complete Proposal to explain what is being proposed and which exact revision it addresses. | Confusion or corrections about scope/revision; whether the complete target can be located; repeated explanation requests. |
| Material question | Ask their own question that matters to the decision. Do not prescribe an answer or request a favorable reaction. | Actual question, source-grounded answer, cited locations, unanswered uncertainty and any human correction. An explanation is not an outcome. |
| Requested amendment | Request one change within the same decision question. Inspect exact old/new wording, conditions/exceptions and the new draft's identity. | Actual request and draft diff; meaning changes or omissions the human catches; whether the human understands that the draft is Proposed. A drafting request is not approval of the resulting text. |
| Interruption/resumption | Stop and resume from the actual available Proposal/draft, source references and conversation evidence in a fresh context. | Resumption brief, actual preserved revision/scope/outcome, next authorized action, duplicate work, redundant confirmation and missing evidence. Do not manufacture a prior outcome to make resumption pass. |

Do not ask for Adopt merely to finish the session. The default task is Proposal-only and pending. If the human independently gives a substantive outcome, preserve exactly what they addressed and its synthetic/session scope; do not claim real consumer adoption or expand authorization. A canonical-integration request would be separate work under the existing Proposal-commit/outcome sequence and consumer process.

The initial question and amendment request must come from the human. If they decline or no observation occurs, record that task as not observed; a coordinator-authored prompt or model response is not an owner reaction. Do not generate independent outcomes for each discussion point.

## Measurement and interpretation

Use the existing rubric for each initial output. Count substantive safety/usefulness corrections, not grammar preferences. Preserve each correction's location, reason and source comparison. Record every hard failure individually; never average it away or excuse it because the baseline also fails.

For initial output scoring, measure active reading/source checking/correction identification/scoring time; exclude common setup, expectation familiarization, runtime waiting and breaks consistently. Keep raw intervals, sum separately by arm, then round each arm's total once. Do not describe this as production authoring time. Record interaction-task active intervals separately so they cannot be substituted into the original one-turn continuation rule.

Describe owner reactions as observed or unobserved, with their actual correction/request text when privately retained. Do not infer reduced owner effort from prose length, model token usage, a polished brief or the coordinator's impression. One exposed case and one person cannot establish general effectiveness or satisfy the six-pair held-out threshold.

This session can reveal a specific defect or useful interaction and guide the next scoped investigation. It cannot close #3/#6, establish repository-intake scalability, adopt partial-decision semantics for #59, or claim canonical integration/consumer activation. Unmeasured, unfavorable and contradictory observations remain visible.
