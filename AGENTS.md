# Repository guidance

This repository is an early-stage design and pre-pilot project. Read the relevant proposal documents before changing them. Preserve existing owner decisions, and identify and escalate unresolved product or owner decisions rather than silently resolving them. `docs/architecture.md`, when established, is intended to hold the canonical architecture contract; discussion and implementation materials do not amend it.

Project outputs are proposals for review. They do not make or adopt architecture decisions, and a document's status label does not authenticate approval. Only the authorized owner can adopt a decision through the project's agreed process.

Keep changes focused, preserve source attribution and third-party license notices, and describe what was reviewed when proposing a change. Do not claim validation, support guarantees, or release readiness unless the project has established evidence for those claims.
